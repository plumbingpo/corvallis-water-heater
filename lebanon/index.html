How Lebanon Homeowners Can Extend Water Heater Life

The best way to avoid a surprise leak is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners flush the tank once a year (more often with hard water), have the anode rod checked every three to five years, test the relief valve annually to make sure it isn't stuck, and keep the thermostat around 120 degrees, which reduces the risk of scalding and cuts down on scale. Tankless owners should plan on descaling once a year if their water is hard.

Age matters too. Most tank water heaters run 8 to 12 years, tankless units often reach 20 with regular descaling, and heat pump models usually run 10 to 15. If your heater is past that window and showing symptoms like discolored hot water, popping and rumbling, moisture around the base, or hot water that runs out sooner than it used to, a planned replacement is nearly always less expensive and much easier on you than an emergency one with water on the floor.

Minor issues don't stay minor for long. Take a spent anode rod: replacing it is a quick, affordable repair, but once it's gone, the water starts eating the tank steel, and suddenly a minor fix has become a full replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to keep our number in their phone and call at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves money and spares you a flooded utility room.

Typical Water Heater Issues in Lebanon

Whatever type of water heater your Lebanon home has, it tends to fail in a predictable way. Gas tanks tend to have trouble with thermocouples and flame sensors, dirty burners, and venting that doesn't draft. Electric tanks burn out heating elements and thermostats, especially when sediment piles up around the lower element. Tankless units throw error codes for scale buildup, ignition failures, and flow sensor problems, most of which trace back to skipped descaling. Heat pump water heaters need clean air filters and a clear condensate drain, and they struggle in cramped closets. We service all four types regularly and carry the parts to fix most problems on the first visit.
